* Scope
The startup leg of ONE run subtree (=Run.Supervisor= -> =Journal.Writer= -> =Run.Server= -> =Work.Supervisor=) under
either owner: the foreground =Run.Executor.Owner= (synchronous caller, =Owner.run/2=) and the mounted
=Host.RunOwner=. In scope: everything from the owner's birth until the subtree is acknowledged as started (or the
startup is abandoned), the owner's and helper's death during that leg, and what the owner reports about ownership
afterwards. Out of scope: the ordinary run (its await stays indefinite), the post-result completion bound (60 s,
unchanged), installed daemon/runtime, CLI routing, live cancellation, any lock deletion, automatic recovery, the
blocked-acquire singleton limit (a blocked arbiter is =ownership_unavailable= for every caller: recorded, not solved).

* 1. Vocabulary and processes
- OWNER: the foreground owner process or the mounted =RunOwner=. It fixes ONE absolute monotonic startup deadline at
  its own birth, before any other process exists: =deadline = birth + budgets.startup=.
- STARTER: the only process that may block. It is the OTP parent of =Run.Supervisor=, retained for the whole run
  (a returning parent ends the supervisor), released only by the teardown. It performs the synchronous start
  (=Owner.start_subtree/1=, the =journal_exists= retry inside) and the DISCOVERY (=Owner.facts/1=) itself.
- HELPER: the responsive reaper. Linked to and monitored by the owner; it ADOPTS the starter - links and monitors it
  as its own first action, and hands it its config - so the starter holds nothing of any reaper's before that. The
  owner spawns the starter UNLINKED inside =begin/2= and therefore never links to it, which is what lets =begin/2=
  return without waiting for a message from either process (the amendment the R3 review finding required: an owner
  must not have a birth gap in front of the clock it is about to arm). An unadopted starter announces nothing and
  expires on the owner's own deadline.
  It never makes a synchronous call. It acknowledges Writer births while the owner lives and, on the owner's DOWN
  or an abort, reaps every known identity.
- Interface (=AiOrchestrator.Run.Executor.Startup=, consumed identically by both owners; the OWNER handles identity,
  permit, expiry and acceptance during the seam's own handshake, so both owners stay responsive and the owner-birth
  deadline covers that interval):
  - =begin(config, budgets)= -> ={:ok, startup}= with =startup = %{ref, helper, starter, deadline, budgets}=; it returns
    at once after spawning the helper (linked+monitored to the caller) and the starter (UNLINKED, adopted by the
    helper), awaiting no message from either; the deadline is
    =birth + budgets.startup= where =birth= is the owner's own monotonic birth time passed in =budgets.birth= (default
    =now=).
  - Events to the owner: ={:startup_identity, ref, starter}= (the starter asks for its permit);
    ={:startup_started, ref, {:ok, sup, facts} | {:error, rejection}}= (completion, delivered by the helper);
    ={:startup_aborted, ref, report}= (a reap the helper performed on its own: owner DOWN); the worker handoff
    ={:run_worker_registered, ref, worker, server}= is unchanged.
  - =permit(startup)= -> =:ok= | ={:error, :late_identity}=: grants only while =now <= deadline= (and the owner's phase
    is the handshake), otherwise denies and aborts through the helper; the owner records the starter before calling it.
  - =accept(startup, completion_event)= -> ={:ok, sup, facts}= | ={:error, %{clause: "run_startup_timeout", why:
    :late_result_after_deadline | :late_error_after_deadline, late_class, ownership, joined}}= | the completion's own
    error: the acceptance-time comparison, collection and teardown of a late tree live here.
  - =abort(startup, why)= -> report (expiry =:deadline=, =:caller_gone=, stop): the helper reaps and joins;
    =reap(startup)= -> report: the owner's mirror duty after a helper DOWN (through the starter it holds);
    =teardown(startup, budgets)= -> =:ok= | ={:error, %{clause: "run_executor_teardown_incomplete", survivors}}= at
    a normal end (the helper runs the shared teardown, releases the starter, both joined).
  - =default_budgets/0= = =%{startup: 45_000, ack: 5_000}=; =Owner.default_budgets/0= gains the same keys.
  - Reports carry =%{why, phase, ownership, joined, survivors}=; =ownership= is the conditional diagnostic of section 5.
- Budget seam (server-owned; a caller cannot set it through =Commands=): the foreground path reads
  =config.opts[:startup_budgets]= (a Host option keyword entry like =:ownership=, dropped from no caller because the
  executor context is built by =Run.Executor.prepare/2=), defaulting to =default_budgets/0=; the mounted path reads
  =Host.mount/3= =budgets:= extended with =:startup= and =:ack=. =Owner.run/2= keeps its =(config, barrier)= shape and
  its synchronous semantics; tests inject short budgets through these seams only.

* 2. Identity before work (the permit)
1. The starter sends ={:starter_identity, ref, self()}= to the owner and performs NO synchronous work until it holds
   ={:permit, ref}=. While waiting it answers its helper's DOWN and the deadline (exit =:normal=, note to the owner,
   nothing acquired).
2. The owner records the starter FIRST (retained for cleanup), then grants the permit ONLY while its phase is
   =:handshake= and =now <= deadline=; otherwise it sends ={:deny, ref}= and aborts startup as
   =run_startup_timeout= with =why: :late_identity=.
3. The starter re-checks the SAME absolute deadline when it consumes the permit, before any synchronous work; an
   expired permit is refused (=:permit_expired=, exit =:normal=). A queued release consumed after the deadline is
   not a release. Neither process trusts a queued message across the clock; both guards are required.
4. After the permit the owner ALWAYS holds the starter identity and owns mirror cleanup through it.

* 3. Writer birth acknowledgment
=Journal.Writer.init/1= with =birth: {reaper, ref}= sends ={:run_writer_born, ref, self()}= to the reaper and WAITS
for ={:run_writer_ack, ref}= BEFORE =acquire_lock=, bounded by =budgets.ack= (5 s). ={:run_writer_abort, ref}= or
silence stops the Writer before any acquire (=writer_birth_aborted= / =writer_birth_unacknowledged= rejections, no
registration, no lock). The reaper (helper) acknowledges only while the owner lives and the phase is
=:handshake= or =:starting=. The worker handoff (=owner_handoff=) is unchanged and still targets the owner.

* 4. Deadline authority
- One clock: handshake, permit, start (both =journal_exists= attempts), discovery and the Writer ack all consume
  =budgets.startup=. No inner discovery budget. =acquire_timeout= stays the Writer-internal inner seam.
- Every completion (success or error) is compared with the absolute deadline at ACCEPTANCE by the owner; accepted
  after the deadline -> =run_startup_timeout= with =why: :late_result_after_deadline | :late_error_after_deadline=
  and =late_class= recorded; the born tree is collected and torn down. Accepted before -> the completion's own
  class (=ownership_unavailable= from the inner seam included). Timer messages are wake-ups, never authority.
- No completion at all -> the owner expires at the deadline (a real =after= / state timeout it arms from
  =startup.deadline=), calls =abort(startup, :deadline)= and reports =run_startup_timeout= with =why: :deadline=, the
  phase (=:handshake= | =:starting=), =ownership= and =joined=; the report is complete only after every join.
- The 60 s post-result completion bound of =Owner.run/2= is unchanged and bounds only the owner's DOWN after its
  reply; ordinary =Run.Server.await= stays indefinite.

* 5. Teardown, survivors and the ownership diagnostic
- Forced teardown (expiry, caller death, abort, stop or parent EXIT during startup) kills the Writer identities
  FIRST (acknowledged and discovered), then the supervisor, then born children, then the starter; every kill is
  joined under =budgets.join=; a survivor is reported as =run_executor_teardown_incomplete= with its count, never
  as success. The run supervisor is FROZEN (scheduler-level suspension) for the Writer window only and killed under
  that freeze: its own reaction to losing a child would otherwise race this accounting and collapse the identities
  the report is about, so the diagnostic below could not rest on observed deaths. This is the same primitive the
  =Host.stop= arbitration uses to make "read the evidence, then act" atomic.
- The reaper's own death establishes NOTHING about the subtree: the starter traps that exit and goes on holding a
  live =Run.Supervisor=. Every owner therefore performs the mirror duty on helper loss in EVERY live phase, and a
  teardown whose helper is already gone - a monitor answering =:noproc= included - reaps through the starter and
  reports what it joined. A missing reaper is never cleanup success. A supervisor-only or helper-only kill is insufficient (a trapping Writer blocked in a filesystem call
  survives its parent's EXIT) and is never the product's mechanism.
- Ownership diagnostic (=ownership:= on every abort/teardown report), observed within a bounded read (500 ms) that
  never blocks the reaper, CONDITIONAL and never asserted: =:none= when no acquire happened (before the birth ack);
  =:reclaimable= when a registered Writer was force-killed (arbiter registration =:down=, or =:live= with a dead
  pid, which the arbiter reclaims on the same terms); =:unknown= when the observation is unavailable or failed.
  The observation is taken AFTER the Writers are killed and joined, and a =:live= registration is reclaimable only
  when it names a Writer this reap has itself joined dead; a registration naming any other identity is =:unknown=.
  An intention to kill is not an observation, so the abort trace follows the deaths it reports and never precedes
  them.
  Placement: the foreground result map for ={:error, run_startup_timeout | run_executor_down}=; the trace message
  ={:run_startup_aborted, owner, diagnostic}= for =:caller_gone= (no receiver); the retained terminal result of a
  mounted owner (=Host.await/2=). =Host.stop/2='s public return shape is unchanged.
- No automatic recovery leg inside teardown and no lock deletion: a forced stop is not a release. Recovery is a
  FRESH command whose Writer reclaims through the arbiter under its own bounded startup; three precise classes:
  empty journal -> =command_run_mismatch=; partial preamble -> =preamble_violation=; admitted run -> the command's
  ordinary outcome (a cancel cancels). "Arbiter =:none= and disk =:none=" is the OBSERVED post-reclaim/close outcome
  of that fresh command (its Writer reclaimed, worked and closed), not a guaranteed globally observable intermediate
  state before the new Writer's work.

* 6. Mounted owner, Host.stop witness, retained terminal
- =Host.RunOwner= consumes the same =Startup=: its =:starting= state is responsive (=inspect/2= answers,
  =stop_request= is acknowledged =:stopping= and torn down under the teardown budgets inside =child_shutdown_ms=,
  waiters and timers are processed). Expiry -> terminal ={:error, run_startup_timeout}= retained; =await= answers
  it; =stop= -> =:retained=. The requester's death never affects the run (mounted lifetime independent).
- Host root shutdown during startup: the tree is gone at the root's stop return, never outliving the arbiter.
- =Host.stop= arbitration witness: the direct owner -> =Run.Supervisor= link is replaced by the THREE-hop chain
  owner -> helper -> starter -> =Run.Supervisor= (=$initial_call= witnesses), true while blocked and while running,
  FALSE at terminal: the owner unlinks and joins helper and starter before entering =:terminal=, so a retained
  owner holds no link to any owned identity. The arbitration keeps its shape (freeze the owner, re-read the chain
  under the freeze, kill only a still-chained silent child of this host's supervisor); helper and starter act only
  on the owner's DOWN, its abort or its teardown, none producible by a frozen owner; a retained result is never
  destroyed on a sampled phase.

* 7. Preserved invariants
Indefinite =Run.Server.await=; synchronous foreground return through the owner's reply and caller monitor only (no
link, no =trap_exit= change, no message consumed); worker-before-effect handoff and acknowledgment to the OWNER; one
Writer per run directory; observed close evidence for ={:ok, map}=; =journal_exists= retry-only admission (both
attempts inside the starter under one clock, byte-identical semantics); stamp/policy path; blocked append/close
drains at the stop budget; the acquire leg's =acquire_timeout= seam (its class wins only when accepted before the
outer deadline); =Host.stop= public shape; retention semantics.
